Build a print-at-home PDF from the SRD. Search for the rules, gear, abilities,
traits and proficiencies your table actually uses, or drop in a whole page as a
ready-made chunk and break it into its parts. Arrange them in any order, set how
each one sits in the heading hierarchy, and print — the browser does the
typesetting, so you get real page breaks and a contents page.
