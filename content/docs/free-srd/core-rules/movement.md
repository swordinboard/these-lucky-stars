# Movement

Movement is the most common action a character takes during any scene — in combat, during exploration, and in any situation where physical position matters. On a character's turn, they may spend AP to move up to their Speed, to step, or to take other movement-related actions.

## Movement Actions

Move and Step are the two primary movement actions. Both are [Standard Actions](/docs/free-srd/core-rules/action-economy/#standard-actions).
