# Action Economy & Movement

This page covers how a character spends their time and effort during a round of combat, and how they move through the world. Action economy describes *what* a character can do; movement describes *how they move* they can go and *how fast*.

## Movement

Movement is the most common action a character takes during any scene — in combat, during exploration, and in any situation where physical position matters. On a character's turn, they may spend AP to move up to their Speed, to step, or to take other movement-related actions.

{{% include "/snippets/rules/movement/movement" "h3" "false" %}}

### Movement Actions

Move and Step are the two primary movement actions. Both are Standard Actions.
