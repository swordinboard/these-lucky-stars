## Vehicles

Statted vehicles — the things the [Vehicle Rules](/docs/free-srd/core-rules/vehicle-rules/)
are about. Vehicles are grouped by setting, the same way creatures and character
options are: the core list holds anything that works in any setting, and each
module adds its own.

The rules a stat block is read against live on that page: size counted in spaces,
speed tiers and the modifiers they carry, driving and boarding, collision damage,
and how a living mount differs from a machine.

A **mount is not a vehicle** here. It is alive, it has its own attributes and
features, and it can refuse an order, so it is statted as a creature under
[Creatures & NPCs](/docs/free-srd/creatures--npcs/) — the vehicle rules reach its
features through the rider's Drive action.
