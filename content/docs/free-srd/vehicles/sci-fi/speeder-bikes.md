# Speeder Bikes

A speeder bike is barely a vehicle — a frame, a repulsor plate, and enough
throttle to outrun the consequences. It hovers a foot off the deck, so it does
not care what the ground is made of, and it will cross a yard faster than
anything else in this section.

The two below share that frame and diverge on **whether the rider is protected**.
Everything the attack variant gains in plate, it pays for in speed.

## Choosing Between Them

**Speeder Bike** is the default and the one a courier or a scout is riding. Open
frame, no cover from any angle, and 12 Object DEF — which means the rider is the
target, not the bike. In exchange it is the fastest thing on the ground here, and
the Dead Rider Switch means losing the rider ends the chase rather than
scattering a runaway machine across the dock.

**Attack Speeder** is the same idea with the rider sealed inside. Full cover, 25
Object DEF, and an Armored Shell that eats collision damage on the pilot's
behalf until the bike is already Damaged. It gives up a chunk of top speed for
that, and it is the only one of the two that can shoot back.

The pairing does the work at a table: the open bike is who the party chases, and
the armored one is what chases them.

## Encounter Notes

**Found** — Courier work, supply depots, perimeter patrols, anywhere with more
distance than road.

**Who Has Them** — Couriers, scouts and outriders ride the open frame. The attack
variant belongs to whoever is paying for a patrol — security contractors, yard
enforcement, and whoever is chasing the party through a cargo bay.

**Riding One Badly** — Neither bike turns the way it accelerates. Turn radius is
size plus speed modifier, so a bike at full throttle needs most of a loading bay
to come about, and the rider who forgets that meets a wall at the speed they were
so pleased about a moment earlier.
