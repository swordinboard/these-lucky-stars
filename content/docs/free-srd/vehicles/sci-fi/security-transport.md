# Security Transport

A security transport is the answer to a simple question: how do you move
something valuable across a station district when everybody already knows you
are moving it? The answer is plate, a locked cell, and a driver on a contract
that does not include heroics.

It is the first vehicle on this list a party is likely to meet from the outside
rather than the inside. Everything about it is built to be difficult to open in
a hurry, and the fastest way through one is almost never the armour.

## Tactics

The driver does not fight and does not stop. Standing orders are to keep moving
toward the nearest secure gate and let the escort deal with whatever is
happening, and a transport that is still rolling has already done its job.
Occupants fire through the ports and stay inside; the moment somebody dismounts
to make a point, the transport has lost the only advantage it has.

The pressure point is the viewports at 16 Object DEF, the wheels, and the
lockout panel — in that order of obviousness and reverse order of usefulness.

## Encounter Notes

**Found** — Payroll runs, prisoner transfers, evidence moves, and any cargo a
corporation would rather not put on a public dock.

**Who Has Them** — Station security, corporate contract outfits, and whoever
took one off them last month and has not repainted it yet.

**Using It Against the Party** — The cell works in both directions. A party that
loses a fight to the escort wakes up in the back of one, and the Holding Cell
entry is written to be escaped from as often as it is broken into.
