# Utility Skiff

The workhorse of every orbital yard and dust-side depot — a flat alloy deck, two
seats behind a stub windscreen, and no pretence of comfort or protection. Nobody
buys one; they accumulate, get repainted, and outlast the company that ordered
them.

**Features**

- **Load Bed** — The rear deck carries 400lbs, or one Medium passenger riding unsecured. An unsecured rider bails, or is thrown, on any collision.
- **Ground Effect** — Hovers a foot off the deck, ignoring difficult terrain less than a foot deep. Cannot climb a grade steeper than roughly 30°.
{{% /statblock %}}

## Driving It

The Drive check is **DEX**. A skiff is steered with a yoke and a throttle rather
than the whole body, which is the call the rules make for cars and trucks.

Acceleration of 40ft/round means five Drive actions to reach the full 200ft, and
it decelerates twice as fast. In practice a skiff spends most of a scene in the
Slow tier and only reaches Moderate on a straight run — which is why its
collision damage matters more than its speed. Three spaces of dice at +0 still
averages 10, and a loaded skiff creeping through a crowd is dangerous.

## Encounter Notes

**Found** — Cargo bays, freight docks, orbital yards, mining camps. Usually
several, usually unlocked, usually with someone's lunch on the deck.

**Who Has Them** — Dock crews, salvage outfits, anyone moving something heavier
than they are. Rarely owned by a character; nearly always borrowed.

**Scaling** — See [Skiffs](/docs/free-srd/vehicles/sci-fi/skiffs/) for the
long-bed Hauler and the stripped Runner, which are this block with size, DEF and
speed traded against each other.
