# Speeder Bike

A speeder bike is barely a vehicle — a frame, a repulsor plate, and enough
throttle to outrun the consequences. It hovers a foot off the deck, so it does
not care what the ground is made of, and it turns as tightly at speed as it does
parked. That last part is what kills people.

## Encounter Notes

**Found** — Courier work, supply depots, anywhere with more distance than
road.

**Who Has Them** — Couriers, scouts, outriders escorting something slower, and
whoever is chasing the party through a cargo bay.
