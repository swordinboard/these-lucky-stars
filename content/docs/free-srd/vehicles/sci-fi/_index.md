# Sci-Fi Vehicles

Transport for spacefaring settings, from the pallet sleds cluttering a dock to
whatever the party is about to steal off it. Each vehicle's stat block is read
against [Vehicle Rules](/docs/free-srd/core-rules/vehicle-rules/) — size in
spaces, speed tiers, and the collision formula in particular.

## Ground & Repulsor

- [Speeder Bike](/docs/free-srd/vehicles/sci-fi/speeder-bike/) — An open-frame repulsor bike, fast and unprotected
- *(TBD)* — An enclosed ground transport, for parties who prefer cover

## Void Craft

Nothing statted yet. Shuttles, haulers and the small craft a party lives out of
belong here.

The parts that go **inside** a vehicle are equipment rather than vehicles, and
stay with the rest of the gear:
[Sci-Fi Equipment](/docs/free-srd/inventory--equipment/sci-fi-equipment/).
