# Sci-Fi Vehicles

Transport for spacefaring settings, from the pallet sleds cluttering a dock to
whatever the party is about to steal off it. Ground and repulsor craft are
statted first, since those are what a party meets on foot.

## Ground & Repulsor

- [Speeder Bike](/docs/free-srd/vehicles/sci-fi/speeder-bike/) — Open frame, one rider, no cover and a great deal of speed
- [Skiffs](/docs/free-srd/vehicles/sci-fi/skiffs/) — The utility workhorse, the long-bed hauler, and the stripped runner
- *(TBD)* — An enclosed ground transport, for parties who would rather have cover

## Void Craft

Nothing statted yet. Shuttles, haulers, and the small craft a party lives aboard
belong here. Those are big enough to need deck plans and crew stations rather
than a single card, so they will arrive after the ground vehicles settle.

The parts that go **inside** a vehicle are equipment rather than vehicles, and
stay with the rest of the gear:
[Sci-Fi Equipment](/docs/free-srd/inventory--equipment/sci-fi-equipment/).
