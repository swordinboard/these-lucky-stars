# Classic Human
<!-- note about the classic human trope -->
Humanity has long been described as a varied and highly diverse race, and tabletop games have long sought to reclect that when defining them because, well, what else would we focus on? The Classic Human pays homage to the role of "generic race with a bit mote options," and is an excellent choice for Human-centric settings as well as those that share the world with other races.

**Traits** — Classic Humans begin with three Trait selections.
