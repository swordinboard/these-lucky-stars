# Station Guard

Dock and corridor security, hired by the berth rather than the year. Every
station has them, no station pays them well, and most of them would rather
write an incident report than fill one out about themselves. Competent with a
baton, unenthusiastic about dying for someone else's cargo manifest.

**Features**

- **Call It In** — 1 AP. Raises the alarm on the coms net. Every other guard within earshot of a speaker rolls Initiative and arrives within 1d4 rounds.
- **Contract Terms** — Surrenders or withdraws once reduced to 1 VIT, or once every other guard present is down. The pay does not cover this.

## Tactics

Opens with the baton, because the paperwork for a discharged firearm is worse
than the beating. Draws the pistol once a second guard is down or a weapon is
already pointed at them. Uses **Call It In** on the first round if the party is
obviously armed — the guard's real weapon is the four more guards arriving on
round three, and a GM running them as a delaying action rather than a threat
gets the most out of them.

They are not fanatics. A party that talks, bribes, or simply outnumbers them
will usually find them willing to look the other way, and a guard who has
surrendered stays surrendered.

## Encounter Notes

**Found** — Dock approaches, cargo bays, habitat checkpoints, anywhere a
station has something worth a lock.

**Organization** — Pairs on patrol, three to six at a checkpoint, plus whoever
answers the coms net.

**Scaling** — A shift supervisor is the same block with FORT 4, a second
attack, and enforcer armor. A bored dockhand with a stolen baton is the same
block with no vest, DEF 5, and no coms.
