### Internal Comp Jack

*–Cr — 2lbs — Hands — Internal*

A retractable interface built into the host's fingertip or palm. Functions as a standard [Comp Jack](/docs/free-srd/inventory--equipment/sci-fi-equipment/sci-fi-misc-equipment/#comp-jack) however no longer requires two hands to operate. Requires [Comp Jack Proficiency](/docs/free-srd/character-creation/proficiencies/sci-fi/#comp-jack-proficiency) to use.

Does not occupy the Hands slot for other equipment.
