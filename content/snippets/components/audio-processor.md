*–Cr — 0lbs — Head — Internal*

Enhanced audio sensors with optional filtering, directional focus, and frequency expansion at the cost of power consumption while leaving base hearing functions available. Each activation costs 0 AP, 1 supply roll of the host's primary power source, and lasts 30 minutes. While active the host gains a +2 bonus on hearing-based INS checks, can focus on specific sounds at will, and can detect frequencies outside the normal range of hearing.

Does not occupy the Head slot for other equipment.
