*--Cr // 1lb // Head [HUD] // Internal*

Integrated optical sensors with low-light, thermal, and zoom modes. The host gains a minor advantage on sight-based INS checks in full and low-light conditions and can see clearly in total darkness out to 100ft. Each activation of the advanced sensors costs 0 AP, 1 supply roll of the host's primary power source, and lasts for 30 minutes. Switching between modes once activated costs 1 AP.

Occupies the HUD subslot for other equipment.
