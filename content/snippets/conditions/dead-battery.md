*Automated Machine Condition*

If an Android's or other automated machine's primary power source is fully depleted at any time, they immediately shut down and are considered unconscious for all purposes. Attacks against a machine in Dead Battery gain a minor advantage and count as surprise attacks. For Androids, all memory since the last successful Rest Mode session is lost upon entering Dead Battery.

*Ending Dead Battery:* Replace or sufficiently recharge the primary power source.
