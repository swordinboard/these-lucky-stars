### Fatigued

*Effect* — The body is tired and actions become sluggish and more difficult to perform. -2 to STR, AGI, and DEX, -2 AP per round. Attackers gain advantage against a fatigued character.

*Duration* — Until rested.

*Healing* — The fatigued condition can be removed with 1hr of rest.
