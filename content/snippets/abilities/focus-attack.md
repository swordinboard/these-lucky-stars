*WILL 2, Level 3*

For 2 AP you may choose to focus on a single opponent, gaining a minor advantage on your next attack against them. Your opponent must be within line of sight when a focus attack is declared, and the effect expires at the end of your next turn if unused or if you lose line of sight, whichever comes first. This ability can be interrupted by distractions or other effects.
