*[Weapon Proficiency](/docs/free-srd/character-creation/proficiencies/#weapon), DEX 2, Level 3*

Your melee and ranged attacks with two weapons are more difficult to defend against. Once per round, spend an additional 2 AP to gain a minor advantage on an attack while using two weapons with which you are proficient.
