Size is often referenced during movement or combat. Living things have somewhat different rules for size than objects such as [vehicles](/docs/free-srd/core-rules/vehicle-rules/#vehicle-size). A creature or character's size affects its attributes, its Grapple and Stealth stats, how much space it takes up during combat, and how attack damage is calculated. While the size chart provides a framework for what to expect within each size category, exceptions do exist.

Most characters are **Medium** size by default. A character's size is typically determined by their species or other character features. Some traits, abilities, or conditions may change a character's effective size.
