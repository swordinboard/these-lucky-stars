---
title: "Size Limitations"
id: rules/size/limitations
category: [core]
type: reference
tags: [size]
---

The size rules provided will cover *most* situations. Moving beyond the scale usually requires special attention and changes made to an individual's stat block to make sense of it. For most settings this will come in the form of creatures smaller than minuscule, such as most normal insects. In such cases the creatures may be treated as swarms for combat purposes, or used in smaller numbers for narrative purposes.
