**Expected Height:** Using the provided measurements one's height is not expected to be more than double the low range of their size category. Exceptions do exist, and the framework provided is intended as a guide to be applied as needed.

**Expected Width / Wingspan:** Expected Width is important when a character or creature is attempting to squeeze through a smaller space, while wingspan can be useful for picturing flying creatures and reach.

**Footprint:** The space(s) one occupies is referred to as their footprint. A creature or character's footprint must remain equal on all sides. This accounts for the ability to turn freely within their own space and makes movement and combat spacing easy to understand on the battle map. Creatures may still be allowed to move through smaller spaces or pack into tighter formations at the GM's discretion when their measured size would make sense for them to do so. See [Movement Through Occupied Spaces](#movement-through-occupied-spaces) for details that may guide when this is appropriate.

**Reach in Combat:** One's reach in combat determines what spaces they can make melee attacks into. Reach is typically equal to the length of one's footprint unless stated otherwise, and extends from the edge of one's footprint in all directions.

> [!NOTE]
> *Reach of Tiny Creatures:* Creatures of tiny (-1) or smaller size have a reach limited to the same space they occupy, and are able to occupy the same space as other creatures.

Reach is important for movement and opportunity attacks during combat. A size 3 creature occupying a 15ft footprint has a reach that extends 15ft, meaning it can make melee attacks at a distance where it is safely out of reach of medium or smaller creatures.

**Attributes and Stat Modifiers:** Size is relative, and therefore has an effect on one's attributes and stats. Typically large creatures hit harder, while smaller ones are a bit harder to hit and to find when hidden. Use the size chart to find the modifiers that affect a character or creature at different sizes.
