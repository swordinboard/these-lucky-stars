**Turning in Place** — Unless stated otherwise, non-mount vehicles cannot rotate in place. Vehicles have a direction orientated footprint, so they must be moving in order to turn.
*Mounts* are an exception because their footprint is treated as a creature's footprint. They are able to turn in place freely as long as there is the space to do so.

**Turn Radius** — Vehicles require at least a number of diagonal spaces equal to their size plus speed modifier to make a 90° turn. The diagonal begins from the square in the forward corner of the vehicle's footprint on the side of the turn and extends in the direction the vehicle is turning. Making a turn of 90+° while moving in the moderate or higher speed tiers requires a Drive check equal to 5 + the speed tier modifier. Tighter turns can be made, usually adding a major or minor disadvantage at the GM's discretion. A vehicle's footprint must be able to pass between all objects while tracing the arc of the turn or it will cause a collision. The GM determines when a turn requires a roll, simply cannot be made, or would result in a collision, based on the space available and the size and speed of the vehicle.
*Mounts* are able to change direction at will while moving (without a required turn radius) but a rider must succeed on an AGI based ride check to prevent being tossed during sudden or aggressive turns. The target for this check is equal to 5 + the mount's speed tier modifier.
