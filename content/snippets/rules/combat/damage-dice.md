Base damage dice are determined by the size of the attacker relative to the defender:

| Attacker vs. Defender | Damage Die |
|---|---|
| Two or more sizes smaller | 1d4 |
| Same size or ±1 size | 1d8 |
| Two or more sizes larger | 1d12 |

**Bonus Dice** — Attack rolls that beat the target by 10 or more gain an additional damage die.

**Extreme Damage** — Damage from attacks made by creatures *two or more sizes larger* than the target is treated as [extreme](/docs/free-srd/core-rules/combat/#damage-sources) damage of its type, and causes an automatic 1 VIT loss per damage die after damage is resolved. This VIT loss occures even if the attack did not reduce DEF to zero, and does not cause DEF to reset.
