Actions in combat are organized by their tactical role: **Aggressive Actions** are actions that present a threat, provoke responses, or advance a character's position offensively. **Defensive Actions** are actions that protect, reposition safely, or prepare for future events. [Readied Actions](/docs/free-srd/core-rules/action-economy/#readied-actions) may be either aggressive or defensive depending on circumstances — a Readied Action to attack is aggressive; a Readied Action to take cover is defensive. This classification matters for rules like Opportunity Attacks, which trigger on aggressive actions.
