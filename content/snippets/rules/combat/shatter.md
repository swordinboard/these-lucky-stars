id: rules/combat/shatter

type: rule
tags: [combat, actions, aggressive-actions]
---

*(3 AP)* — Used to attempt to break an item held or worn by an opponent, or target a specific part of an object when applicable. Make a standard attack roll; if successful, deal damage to the item's DEF rather than to the opponent. See [Damaged & Broken Gear](/docs/free-srd/core-rules/damaged--broken-gear/) for rules on damaged and broken gear.
