*Effect* — The body is completely unable to move. Though not unconscious, an immobilized character cannot take any physical actions. DEX and AGI are treated as null, attackers gain a major advantage and may make surprise attacks even if they normally would not be able to.

*Duration/Healing* — Cause specific.
