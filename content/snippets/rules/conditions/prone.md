---
title: "Prone"
id: rules/conditions/prone
category: [core]
type: rule
tags: [condition]
summary: "Half speed, minor disadvantage on melee attacks — adjacent attackers gain minor advantage"
duration: "Until standing"
---

*Effect* — Characters that are lying upon the ground are Prone. Prone characters can only move at half speed, and are at a minor disadvantage when making melee attacks outside of a grapple. Attacks made by an adjacent enemy that target a prone character gain a minor advantage.

*Duration/Healing* — Until the character stands, usually with the Stand action.
