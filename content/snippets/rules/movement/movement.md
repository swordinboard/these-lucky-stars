When moving, regardless of what action results in movement, a few standard rules apply to creatures and characters:

**Turning in Place** — Creatures and characters can turn in place freely. Those that occupy multiple spaces must have enough room for their footprint to rotate , often requiring adjacent spaces be clear for uneven footprints such as a 1x2 footprint.

**Turn Radius** — Normally creatures and characters, including mounts, are able to change direction at will while moving. A rider must succeed on an AGI based ride check to prevent being tossed during sudden or aggressive turns. The target for this check is equal to 6 + the mount's speed teir modifier.

Vehicles and mounts follow a modified version of these rules described in the [Vehicles](path) section.
