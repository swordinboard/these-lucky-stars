> **Primary Speed = 20 + 5 per +1 AGI Bonus or Flat −5 if AGI Penalty + Mods**

A primary speed describes the normal mode of movement of a creature or character, conveniently rounded to 5ft spaces. It represents the distance in feet a creature or character can cover with a single Move action.

Primary speeds are calculated using the formula above. A character with AGI bonuses gains a +5 bonus to their primary Speed per AGI bonus. A character with any AGI penalty takes a flat −5 penalty to their primary Speed, regardless of how negative the penalty is.

Secondary speeds are usually slower than the primary speed and are provided by a character's race selection or a creatures description.

A Vehicle's speed is provided in its description.
