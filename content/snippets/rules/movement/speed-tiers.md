Many rules reference a current **speed tier**. Speed tiers describe how fast something is moving at the time another object or affect acts on it. Speed tiers are universal across all vehicles and characters. Each tier carries a **speed modifier** used as a flat bonus to collision damage and to determine eligibility and roll targets for different interactions. For example, Opportunity Attacks cannot be made against those moving at the **Fast** and **Very Fast** tiers.

*One's current Speed tier is determined by how far their most current movement takes them in one round.* In almost all cases this is determined by a Move or Drive action where the continuation of that move is assumed or predetermined.

Speed tiers apply to any moving target, not only vehicles. A character on foot at sprint using special abilities may push into Moderate or higher speed. The tier vocabulary is the same regardless of what is moving.
