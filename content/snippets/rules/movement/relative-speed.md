---
title: "Relative Speed"
id: rules/movement/relative-speed
category: [core]
type: rule
tags: [movement]
---

When two objects or creatures moving in opposite directions collide or otherwise act on one another, the result may be based on a speed tier that is relative to one another.  The simple method is to subtract their speeds when moving in the same or similar direction, and to add their speeds when moving in opposing directions. Both sides of the action then use the new speed tier value to calculate collision damage, action targets, or whatever else may be necessary.

Using this method makes it so two vehicles colliding head on take increased damage, or two characters fighting each other on the back of adjacent carts moving at the same speed see no change in advantage.

This method can become complicated when judging angled or perpendicular impacts, leaving judgement to the GM as to whether such a situation requires relative speed or is best settled with normal speed tiers.
