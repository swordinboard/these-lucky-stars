---
title: "Movement Through Occupied Spaces"
id: rules/movement/occupied-spaces
category: [core]
type: rule
tags: [movement, size]
---

Creatures and characters within two size categories in either direction cannot pass through a space occupied by an enemy, but can pass through allies' spaces.

Any creature whose footprint is less than a full space may move through spaces occupied by both allies and enemies and may share a space with them at the end of their movement (still provoking Opportunity Attacks as normal). This contradicts normal movement rules for other size categories, but is integral to combat movement and flow for tiny creatures due to their limited reach. The number of tiny creatures that may occupy one space at any time alongside a larger creature in this manner is normally 4, but exceptions may be made at the GM's discretion.

Similar to tiny creatures, those that are three or more size categories larger or smaller than another may pass through and/or end on the opposite creature's space without penalty — essentially presenting the same situation where the space is able to be shared — so long as no more than half of the spaces within the larger creature's footprint are affected at once (rounded down, min 1).

{{% quickref "Example of Shared Spaces" %}}
A large (2) creature can move through the spaces of two tiny (-1) creatures that are occupying separate spaces, or possibly more if the tiny creatures share a space. If two tiny creatures share a space, and a third stands in a space next to them, the large creature would still be able to move through that space because the tiny creatures only occupy two of the four spaces that make up the large creature's footprint.
{{% /quickref %}}
