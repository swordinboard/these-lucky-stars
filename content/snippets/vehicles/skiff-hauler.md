**Features**

- **Long Bed** — Carries 1,600lbs, or four Medium passengers riding unsecured. Anything unsecured bails, or is thrown, on any collision.
- **Ground Effect** — Hovers a foot off the deck, ignoring difficult terrain less than a foot deep. Cannot climb a grade steeper than roughly 20° loaded.
- **Committed** — Low maneuverability and a five-space footprint mean it neither turns out of trouble nor fits through it. Loaded, it is often easier to drive through an obstacle than around it — which the collision dice make viable.
