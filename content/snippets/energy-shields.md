Energy shields create temporary semi-transparent barriers that absorb damage on behalf of the wearer similar to physical shields. When activated energy shields grant a temporary DEF bonus that is depleted before normal DEF. Once the temporary DEF pool is depleted, any remaining damage applies to the defender's DEF as normal. Unlike normal DEF, temporary DEF does not reset. Energy shields must be depleted or turned off to reset, with a new activation follwoing the device's supply rules.
Objects and creatures cannot pass through an energy shield while it is active, offering a form of full cover to those on opposite sides, however, those on opposite sides can still target eachother and damage the shield until it is depleted.
Due to the high energy demand, energy shields often have few uses.
