The vessel that arrives when the interceptor has finished telling everyone where
you are. Forty-five feet of spaced laminate around a troop bay and a docking
collar, slow enough to be outrun and armoured enough that outrunning it is the
only option on the table.

>
> **Occupants** pilot + 7 // **Cover** full (enclosed)

>

>

**Features**

- **Dorsal Turret** — 3 AP, fired by a gunner who is not the pilot and therefore takes **no** driving disadvantage — the difference between this and an interceptor's nose guns is a whole crew position. Range 400ft, full 360° arc, [Ballistic [1d12]](/docs/free-srd/inventory--equipment/item-tags/#ballistic).
- **Spaced Laminate** — Just under two inches of hard plate in two layers with a gap between them, for Object DEF 35 and the Durable frame behind it. A gunship is meant to still be flying at the end of whatever it flew into.
- **Troop Bay** — Five seats, a rack, and a rear ramp that drops for 2 AP. Anyone in the bay is [boarding](/docs/free-srd/core-rules/vehicle-rules/#boarding--bailing) a stationary target the moment the ramp is down, and a bay full of people is the actual weapon here.
- **Docking Collar** — Two rounds at 2 AP to mate against a hull and cut. From then on the two vessels are one space for movement, and everyone aboard either is one door from the other.
