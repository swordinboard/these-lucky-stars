Eighty feet of hull built around the assumption that nothing will be coming to
help. Twelve bunks, a hangar bay, a reclamation plant that works, and enough
consumables that the crew will get bored long before they get hungry. It is a
place to live that happens to move.

**Features**

- **Never Lands** — Built and assembled in orbit, and structurally incapable of entering an atmosphere. Everything that touches a surface goes down in the lander, which makes the hangar bay the ship's front door and its single point of failure.
- **Hangar Bay** — Holds one craft of Size 8 or smaller — a [Survey Lander](#survey-lander) is the intended fit. Launch or recovery is a full round at 4 AP with the bay depressurized, and the bay cannot be pressurized and open at the same time.
- **Two Years Out** — Consumables, reclamation and air for twelve crew for two years. An explorer does not resupply; it *returns*, and the difference between those two words is most of the drama in any campaign built on one.
- **Deep-Range Hull** — An inch and a half of hard plate against micrometeorite and radiation, for Object DEF 30 and a Durable frame. It is not armour and it was never meant to be, but it is a great deal of hull.
- **The Long Watch** — Only two crew stations are ever manned underway. A boarder who reaches the hull unobserved has the run of ten of the twelve compartments before anyone is expected to notice.
