Ninety feet of hull that has been sold eleven times and repaired badly on nine
of those occasions. A hold, a galley, six bunks, and a registry that is accurate
in most of the ports that matter. This is the ship a party owns, which is to say
the ship a party owes money on.

**Features**

- **The Hold** — A hundred tons, a cargo ramp, and enough unlisted volume behind the deck plates to lose about two tons of anything a manifest should not mention. Finding it is a target 14 INS check for anyone who knows to look.
- **Patched Plate** — Object DEF 18 of soft metal, in about forty pieces. It has been holed and welded so many times that no two panels are the same thickness, and the GM is invited to decide that the shot went through one of the thin ones.
- **Temperamental** — On a cold start, the third round of ignition needs a target 10 KNO check from whoever is at the panel. On a failure the sequence aborts and must begin again, which is two more rounds, and it always happens in the round somebody starts shooting.
- **Lives Aboard** — Six bunks, a galley, water reclamation, and air for six for four months. A freighter crew is not visiting the ship; the ship is the address.
- **Unarmed, Nominally** — No mounts as delivered. Both dorsal hardpoints are present, capped, and wired, because every hull in this class was built expecting somebody to get around to it.
