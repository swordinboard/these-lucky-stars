Forty feet of blunt hull on four splayed legs, built to come down somewhere with
weather and go back up again afterwards. Half the interior is bunks and the
other half is instruments, and the argument about which half should be larger
has been running for as long as the design has existed.

**Features**

- **Made for Weather** — Built to fly in air as readily as outside it. Descent through a storm, a dust front or a corrosive sky needs no Drive check that clear air would not also need.
- **Field Lab** — A sealed bench with a [Bio Scanner](/docs/free-srd/inventory--equipment/sci-fi-equipment/sci-fi-misc-equipment/#bio-scanner) rig and sample containment. Analysis performed here rather than by hand grants a *minor advantage* on KNO checks to identify a sample, and takes an hour rather than a round.
- **Six Weeks Out** — Consumables, water reclamation and air for six crew for six weeks. Endurance is the thing a lander sells; there is no resupply written into any scenario it is used in.
- **Landing Legs** — Four splayed legs let it set down on a grade up to roughly 20° or on ground that will not take a wheel. Deploying or stowing them is 2 AP, and a lander that lifts with them down does not lift far.
- **Unarmed** — No mounts, no hardpoints, no turret. A survey lander that meets something hostile has exactly the options its Speed line gives it.
