**Features**

- **Dead Rider Switch** — If the rider leaves the saddle for any reason — bailing, thrown, dead — the throttle cuts and the bike decelerates to a stop rather than carrying on under power. A passenger that does not fall off with the driver can take the controls, but the deceleration effect is immediate.
- **Repulsor Bike** — Hovers a foot off the deck or ground and automatically adjusts height based on upcoming obstacles, ignoring difficult terrain and objects up to two feet high or deep, but anything above that height results in a collision. Cannot climb a grade steeper than roughly 45°.
- **Steering Vanes** — The two forward vanes are more fragile than the main body. An attack that deliberately targets the vanes is a target 10 + speed modifier [Shatter](/docs/free-srd/core-rules/combat/#aggressive-actions). Vanes that take 1 VIT of damage in this manner, or from a front end collision, cause the maneuverability to drop. This causes turns to take twice as many spaces as they would normally require until repaired.
