**Features**

- **Armored Shell** — Roughly an inch of hard metal plating around the repulsor frame, trading cover and increased DEF for a lower max speed. The shell absorbs collision consequence on the pilot's behalf: the pilot takes no damage frolkm a collision unless the bike is already at 1 VIT (Damaged). Below that, collisions resolve normally against the pilot as well
- **Forward Lasers** — Two mounted lasers fixed in a forward position protruding from the armored shell. Fires in a straight line directly ahead of the vehicle with a range of <!--range insert-->. Attacks are made by the pilot, are DEX based, and count as medium for damage purposes. The weapons are direct wired to save on weight, so each attack requires one supply roll of the vehicle's power source.
- **Repulsor Bike** — Hovers a foot off the deck or ground and automatically adjusts height based on upcoming obstacles, ignoring difficult terrain and objects up to two feet high or deep, but aything above that height results in a collision. Cannot climb a grade steeper than roughly 45°.
