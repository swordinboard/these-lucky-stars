**Features**

- **Armored Shell** — Roughly an inch of hard metal plating over a roll cage creates a tight enclosed cockpit over the saddle and steering vanes, trading a lower max speed for cover and increased DEF. The shell absorbs collision consequence on the pilot's behalf: the pilot takes no damage from a collision until the bike reaches 1 VIT (Damaged). Below that, collisions resolve normally against the pilot as well.
- **Forward Lasers** — Two mounted lasers are fixed in a forward position protruding from the armored shell, and can be fired in a straight line directly ahead of the vehicle with a normal range of 100ft. Attacks are made by the pilot, are DEX based, and count as medium-sized weapons for damage purposes. The weapons are direct wired to save on weight, so each attack requires one supply roll of the vehicle's power source.
- **Repulsor Bike** — Hovers a foot off the deck or ground and automatically adjusts height based on upcoming obstacles, ignoring difficult terrain and objects up to two feet high or deep, but anything above that height results in a collision. Cannot climb a grade steeper than roughly 45°.
