**Features**

- **Crashproof** — A collision with an obstacle smaller than the crawler never force it to a halt. It deals its collision damage, takes half, and continues along the driver's declared path. Only something of comparable size or larger ends its turn for it. (Half damage is extended to passengers when applicable.)
- **Blind Spot** — The cab sees nothing within 20ft of the hull on any side. A character who reaches that band is invisible to the driver, who cannot react to them at all — which is how most people get aboard one of these.
- **Cold Start** — Ignition takes two full rounds at 2 AP a round: one to bring the reactor up, one to release the track brakes. Clear to move on the third round. A crawler cannot be started in a hurry, and everybody who plans to steal one knows that going in.
