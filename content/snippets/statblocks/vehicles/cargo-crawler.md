Fifty feet of tracked chassis under six container clamps, crawling a fixed
circuit between the landing field and the yard. Nothing about it is quick and
nothing about it is subtle. It is the largest object most parties will ever have
the opportunity to steal.

**Features**

- **Tracked Chassis** — Ignores difficult terrain entirely and climbs a grade up to roughly 45°. It cannot be bogged, mired or tipped by anything smaller than itself, and it does not care what the ground is made of.
- **Does Not Stop** — A collision with an obstacle smaller than the crawler never forces it to a halt. It deals its collision damage, takes none back, and continues along the driver's declared path. Only something of comparable size or larger ends its turn for it.
- **Blind Hull** — The cab sees nothing within 20ft of the hull on any side. A character who reaches that band is invisible to the driver, who cannot react to them at all — which is how most people get aboard one of these.
- **Container Clamps** — Six standard clamps along the deck. Releasing one is 2 AP from the cab and drops the container into the crawler's own path, where it becomes difficult terrain the width of two spaces.
- **Cold Start** — Two full rounds at 2 AP a round: one to bring the reactor up, one to release the track brakes. A crawler cannot be started in a hurry, and everybody who plans to steal one knows that going in.
