**Features**

- **Armour Plate** — An inch and a half of hard plate over the cab and the cell, which is where the Object DEF of 30 comes from. The forward viewports are the exception at 16 Object DEF, and everyone who has ever attacked one of these knows it.
- **Firing Ports** — Four slots, two per side. An occupant may attack through one without giving up [full cover](/docs/free-srd/core-rules/combat/#cover), at a *minor disadvantage* for the limited arc. A port cannot be fired through and seen through by two different people at once.
- **Holding Cell** — The rear compartment seats six on bolted benches and locks from the cab. Opening it from the inside is a target 16 STR check, or a [Comp Jack](/docs/free-srd/inventory--equipment/sci-fi-equipment/sci-fi-misc-equipment/#comp-jack) bypass against the door panel.
- **Crew Lockout** — Ignition is 2 AP for anyone carrying a valid crew token. Without one it is three rounds of work at 2 AP a round and a target 16 KNO check with a [Comp Jack](/docs/free-srd/inventory--equipment/sci-fi-equipment/sci-fi-misc-equipment/#comp-jack); a failure locks the panel for ten minutes and signals the operator.
- **Wheels, Not Repulsors** — Too heavy for a repulsor plate. Unlike a skiff it crosses difficult terrain the hard way, and loose ground, ice or rubble calls for a Drive check at the GM's discretion.
