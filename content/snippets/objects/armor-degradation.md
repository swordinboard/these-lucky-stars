---
title: "Armor Degradation"
id: objects/armor-degradation
category: [core]
type: rule
tags: [objects, armor]
---

Equipped armor degrades in condition whenever the wearer suffers a major wound. Functional armor becomes *Damaged* when the wearer suffers a major wound, and Damaged armor becomes *Broken* in the same manner (VIT is adjusted accordingly for each condition). *Damaged* armor functions as normal but visibly shows its near-broken condition. *Broken* armor provides no DEF bonus.

Armor cannot be targeted by the Shatter action, except where a specific weapon or effect states otherwise.
