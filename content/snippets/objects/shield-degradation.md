title: "Shield Degradation"
id: objects/shield-degradation

Shields use supply dice to represent wear from use. A supply roll is made each time the wielder's DEF is depleted from damage while using a shield to defend themselves. When a shield reaches a supply of 1d4 it is *Damaged.* When its supply is depleted it is *Broken.* *Damaged* shields function as normal, but visibly show their near-broken condition and take more work to repair. *Broken* shields cannot be used to defend oneself and may better off discarded until repairs can be made.

Shields can be targeted by the Shatter action. If successful, rather than dealing damage directly, the shield's supply die size is automatically lowered by one and half of the total damage is transferred to the defender as normal.
