*--Cr — 4lbs — [Battery](/docs/free-srd/inventory--equipment/item-tags/#battery) [Small] — [Two-Handed](/docs/free-srd/inventory--equipment/item-tags/#two-handed)*

Fires a magnetic grappling hook that can puncture soft materials, hook an edge, or attach to magnetic surfaces. Successfully deployed lines can be detached from the gun and anchored to provide advantage to related climb checks. The line can support up to 400lbs. Requires 1 supply roll per use. Costs 2 AP to fire, 0 AP to detach a fired line, and 2 AP to anchor a detached line.
