*[General Technology](/docs/free-srd/character-creation/proficiencies/sci-fi/#general-technology) Proficiency, KNO 3*


You can spend 8 AP and a use of the Tech Kit with supplies to install a battery of up to two sizes smaller or larger than required into a piece of equipment. Items accepting large or industrial batteries inherit the [Bulky](/docs/free-srd/inventory--equipment/item-tags/#bulky) item tag.

In addition, two batteries of the same size (excluding large and industrial batteries) may be chained together to act as a battery of one size larger using 4 AP and the Tech Kit with supplies.

Results of fit, placement, and object handling, may vary by combination.

*Normal: Battery cores cannot be interchanged with those of a different size.*
