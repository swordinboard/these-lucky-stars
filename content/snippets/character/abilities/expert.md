**Expert [___]**

*[Student](#student), KNO 2*

Choose one of the topics selected by your Student ability. When applying the Student ability using that topic, add your KNO bonus rather than the +1 bonus provided, whichever is higher.

This ability can be selected a second time to be applied to a second Student ability.
