*[Lucky](/docs/free-srd/character-creation/traits/#lucky) Trait*

You are a beacon of good fortune to your friends. As long as you have at least 1 Luck point remaining, any time an ally uses their final Luck point within 30ft of you they have a 50% chance to regain one Luck point at the end of their action.
